**ABA Therapy Goal Bank**

Tiered Short-Term Goals with Medical Necessity Rationales

*18 skill domains · 3 tiers per domain · Formatted to funder authorization standards*

## **How to use this bank**

Every goal below is written in the standard authorization format and closes with the standard mastery criterion. Bracketed fields mark data points that must be supplied before submission — client name, baseline values, target counts, and operational definitions. Do not submit a goal with an unresolved bracket.

Tiers are functional, not age-based. Select the tier that matches the learner's current repertoire as documented in the most recent assessment, and use the adjacent tier to write the subsequent authorization period's goal.

Rationale language alternates between the two established patterns so that no two goals in the same report read identically. Where a report includes multiple goals from the same domain, vary the pattern and rewrite at least one clause — duplicated rationale language across goals is a frequent audit finding.

## **Standard goal format**

*[Client] will [target behavior with specific criteria] in 80% of opportunities with accuracy and independence across 5 consecutive sessions with 2 people within the six-month authorization period.*

## **Rationale patterns**

Pattern A — Goal-first, three sentences. Opens by naming what the goal targets, cites baseline data in the second sentence, and closes on benefit and generalization. Use for goals where acquisition is the argument.

Pattern B — Skill-first, two sentences. Opens by naming the skill and its function, and closes on the risk or consequence of the skill remaining absent. Use for safety, behavior reduction, and any goal where the absence of the skill is the stronger argument.

## **Reviewer flags**

*Items in this style appear throughout the bank. They are notes to you, not report content — delete every one before submission.*
