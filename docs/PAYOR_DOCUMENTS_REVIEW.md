### Gaps in the current app

- SectionKey is a fixed 11-value union in src/lib/intake/schema.ts. It needs expanding to the new keys, plus support for conditional sections (school_plan, telehealth_readiness, group_goals) that are included only when triggered by requested codes or settings.
- No per-payor section order, titles or template mapping exists, so the Maryland Medicaid enforced order cannot be produced. plan_content rules need a section_order param, and payor templates should be data (seed), not code.
- Missing standalone sections: diagnosis_and_referral, direct_observation (with per-domain severity), goal_summary (status counts and response to treatment), behavior_intervention_plan as distinct from reduction goals, generalization_maintenance, barriers_to_treatment, medical_necessity split from services, anticipated_schedule, prior utilization, and signatures_attestation.
- Goal records lack required fields: date of introduction, projected mastery date, baseline date and setting, goal status (in progress, mastered, on hold, discontinued, new), current-data date, per-goal barriers, setting tag for per-setting data, ABA strategy, and a replacement-behavior flag. Status counts and data-age checks cannot be computed without them.
- No graphing requirements are implemented: per-goal skill line graphs, reduction graphs with phase-change lines, GA's 2:3 ratio, three-series maximum and initials+date label, and equal-interval x axes. Graph labels must be system-filled; the initials are PHI-derived, so they never go to the AI.
- No data-recency validators exist for the payor windows (Cigna 60 days, GA 2 months, Aetna 12 months, TennCare 30 days, Cigna interim 6 months) or for diagnosis-age triggers (GA re-evaluation, SHP 3-year severity reassessment, TRICARE 2-year referral renewal).
- The caregiver_goals_two item is hard-coded. It should be a payor-parameterized min/max (GA 2-4, SHP advisory). A check that goals measure caregiver behavior and a computed caregiver participation percentage from session records are also missing.
- The requested-codes grid lacks per-payor request basis (week/month/auth), unit formulas, a rendering credential column, place of service, telehealth row-combining rules, covered-code lists and a daily cap check. These figures must flow through the numeric firewall as sourced tokens.
- The intake schema is missing restricted fields surfaced by payor forms: trauma history, risk/suicidality assessment, substance use, a depression screen at 13+, and safety plan. All must be tagged sensitivity: restricted and be BCBA-authored only.
- The assessment section lacks structured fields for respondent, form type and edition, the clinician-tool plus caregiver-tool split (GA), payor outcome measures (TRICARE PDDBI/PSI/SIPA/Vineland-3/SRS-2; SHP ABAS/Vineland), and functional impairment in SD units (Aetna).
- There is no exported-PDF per-page client identifier header, and no PDF-only export enforcement for MD.
- There are no conditional content blocks for 0373T, the Cigna severe behavior program, feeding/toileting, clinic-majority generalization/titration (MD), or adult-services transition near age 21.
- There is no attestation model (SHP LBA family-agreement and staffing attestation), no caregiver/LAR signature capture, no consent-for-treatment or release tracking, and no state behavior-analyst licensure in the credential model (GA, TN, TX LBA requirements).
- The seed rule ga_medicaid plan_content must_include lists 6 items against the 12 in the Q4 checklist, and it still cites the Q3 manual.

### Goal format findings

- Mastery criteria are required for every goal by GA, MD (Carelon/Optum), Cigna, SHP, Aetna and Regence. Cigna also requires the degree of independence and that the units of the data, goal and mastery criteria match. Our structured mastery fields (accuracy, sessions, instructors, settings) fit, but they need a unit/measure field shared with the baseline and progress data, plus a validator that all three use the same measure.
- Baseline must be quantitative and dated for every target, and Cigna wants it per setting. SHP also asks for the baseline setting and says baseline values never change once recorded, so baseline should be immutable after approval, with a separate current-data field. GA and Cigna require baselines for new goals to be recent (within the payor window before the goal is introduced). GA requires probe data no older than 2 months for goals that are baselined but not yet introduced.
- Date of introduction and projected mastery date are per-goal fields in GA, Optum and Carelon. TennCare asks for an anticipated timeline and an estimated mastery date. Our 'initiation window' (Months 1-3/4-6) is not enough. Store actual dates as sourced values and render them as tokens.
- Progress reporting must use the same unit as the baseline (GA, Optum, Carelon), include a narrative of teaching-procedure changes (GA, Optum), and include per-goal barriers with actions taken (Carelon). Cigna asks for baseline, interim (covering at least the prior 6 months) and current data. Line graphs per goal (not per target) are required on GA continued requests, and graphs are required for reduction goals by GA, Carelon and SHP.
- A per-goal medical necessity rationale tied to core ASD deficits is required by GA and Carelon, and TRICARE limits targets to core ASD symptoms. Patterns A/B in GOAL_FORMAT.md should be updated to name the core deficit explicitly, and Pattern A's baseline sentence must use tokens only (numeric firewall).
- Goal status is needed for goal summaries (Carelon: total, mastered, in progress, on hold, discontinued, new; SHP: included, met, added, discontinued). Add a status field with dated transitions.
- Exclusions to enforce as warnings or blocks from payor rules: no vocational goals (GA, MD, Cigna, TRICARE), no educational/academic goals (TRICARE, Cigna), no duplication of OT/SLP/PT or school services (GA, MD, Regence), no individual targets presented as goals (Carelon), and no general ADL goals (SHP). Goal bank domains need payor-exclusion tags.
- Required goal categories vary. GA, MD and SHP organize skill goals as language/communication, social and adaptive/self-care. Regence uses six HCA domains. Our 'communication; social and play' list should add adaptive/self-care, and categories should be grouped per payor at render time.
- Caregiver goals must measure caregiver behavior. Attendance, 1:1 BCBA meetings, watching RBT sessions or videos do not count (GA, Carelon, Optum, SHP). They use the same fields as client goals (baseline, introduction date, projected mastery, progress). Count requirements: GA 2-4, SHP 2 or more encouraged, Regence never NA. Cigna requires data separated per stakeholder. Group caregiver goals are separate if 97157 is requested.
- Behavior reduction goals require a paired BIP with operational definition, function, functionally equivalent replacement behavior, antecedent and consequence strategies, and de-escalation (GA, MD, SHP). Optum asks for an explicit replacement-behavior flag on skill goals. Our replacement_paired check is consistent with this.
- Generalization targets: SHP asks for at least two settings, caregivers and typically developing peers. MD asks for people, environments and stimuli. Our default '2 people' should be extended to settings and peers, kept structured, and rendered via tokens.
- Short- and long-term objectives (GA, Regence titled objectives under broad goals) and a maintenance plan per goal (GA) support our existing phases model. Regence also wants plain-language wording readable by non-behavioral readers and per-goal discharge criteria.
- The standard goal sentence embeds digits (80%, 5 sessions, 2 people, six-month). Under the numeric firewall these must render from stored mastery and auth tokens, never as literal digits in AI text. 'Six-month authorization period' should also be a token, because auth length differs by payor (e.g., GA assessment PAs run 3 months).

### Payor-specific notes

- Maryland Medicaid (Carelon) requires its own template, sections in its exact order (a reordered plan is returned), checkboxes kept in place, the BIP inside the plan, and PDF submission (no Word). Its order is: client info, biopsychosocial, narrative, goal summary, skill goals, BIP, reduction goals, caregiver, generalization, transition/fading, discharge, crisis, recommendations (medical necessity, barriers, codes, prior utilization), schedule, provider info, telehealth checklist. The section renderer therefore needs a per-payor order and title map. plan_content needs a section_order param.
- GA Medicaid (Q4 2026 manual) has 12 Plan of Care checklist items in Appendix D. The seed rule lists 6, so it is missing medications, parent concerns, assessment results, separate skill/behavior/caregiver goals and the telehealth checklist. It also needs re-citing from the Q3 version to Q4.
- GA Medicaid graph rules: line graphs at a 2:3 height-to-width ratio, at most three series per graph, x axis labeled with session dates, phase-change lines, and assessment graphs labeled with member initials and an mm/dd/yyyy date. Skill acquisition line graphs are new in Q4 and required on continued requests. The SVG renderer needs payor graph presets.
- Caregiver goal counts differ: GA requires 2-4, a maximum our check does not enforce. SHP encourages at least two, so it should only warn. Regence says the section may not be NA. MD requires caregiver training but sets no count. Our hard-coded caregiver_goals_two item should become a payor-parameterized rule.
- Data recency windows differ: Cigna wants assessment and baseline within 60 days before treatment start, current data within 60 days of both the continuation start and request receipt, interim data covering 6 months, and a standardized assessment within 1 year for reauth. GA wants data and reassessment no more than 2 months before the PA effective date. Aetna wants a standardized functional assessment within 12 months showing impairment of at least 1 SD. TennCare wants a plan dated within 30 days of start, review every 6 months, and standardized re-evaluation every 2 years.
- Outcome measures: TRICARE East requires PDDBI, PSI/SIPA, Vineland-3 and SRS-2 at baseline before treatment starts, then PDDBI plus PSI/SIPA every 6 months and Vineland-3 plus SRS-2 yearly. SHP requires ABAS or Vineland every 6 months with the same rater, and baseline values never change. Aetna maps standard-deviation severity per domain to hours guidance.
- Goal content exclusions: TRICARE and Cigna exclude educational/academic and vocational goals, and TRICARE limits targets to core ASD symptoms. GA, Optum and Carelon exclude vocational goals and goals that duplicate other services. Carelon forbids presenting individual targets as goals. SHP excludes general ADL goals, which may conflict with our adaptive/self-care domain and needs BCBA review.
- Units and codes: GA uses hours x 4 x weeks with 13- or 26-week periods. Carelon uses hours x 4 x 26 per week or hours x 4 x 6 per month (the per-month factor is ambiguous; ask a BCBA). Carelon combines GT/U2 telehealth rows. SHP requests weekly hours and days per week with HO/HN modifiers and caps direct codes at 8 hours/day. TRICARE ACD covers only 97151, 97153 and 97155-97158, with telehealth only for 97156 after the first 6-month auth. GA forbids billing 97154 and 97158 concurrently, while Anthem allows 97155 with 97153 when both are face to face.
- Supervision differs by payor: Cigna sets 1-2 hours per 10 direct hours (direct plus indirect), with a weekly floor when direct hours are 10/week or fewer, and the supervisor must be named. Aetna allows 1-2 hours of 97155 per 10 hours of 97153. MD Medicaid treats 10-20% as typical, with a rationale required above 20%. GA defers to the BACB and requires some in-person supervision.
- Signatures: GA accepts secure e-signatures (no rubber stamps), and the cover sheet points to 602.3.4. SHP requires LBA and LAR signatures plus a family-agreement attestation. Regence requires lead therapist, assistants and parent signatures. Aetna requires every entry dated with signature and credentials. Optum/Carelon have a provider-only signature block.
- Conditional sections by payor: a school plan when services are in a school (GA required, Cigna service description, TRICARE limits who renders). Telehealth readiness (GA and MD checklist; MD also needs consent). Group goals when 97154/97157 is requested (GA, MD, TennCare group size). 0373T content (GA). Feeding/toileting and severe behavior program content (Cigna). Clinic generalization and titration when more than half of services are in a clinic (MD Carelon). Adult-services transition near the benefit age limit (MD).
- Aetna-specific items: client identifier on every page, signed consent and release, written PCP communication at initial evaluation, major change and termination, risk assessment and substance use items (restricted, BCBA-only), and California-only ASD items. Aetna's ABA submission channel conflicts between documents (phone in the manual vs Availity on the newer form).
- SHP: request-type checkbox, depression screen at age 13+ and trauma-history question (both restricted, never sent to AI), ASD severity reassessment when the diagnosis is more than 3 years old, intensity tiers with criteria (the maintenance tier criteria look erroneous, so log it in QUESTIONS_FOR_BCBA.md), and 85% LAR participation considered at renewal (must be computed from session records).
- TRICARE East: referral and DSM-5 checklist renewed every 2 years, specialist diagnosis required if first diagnosed at age 8+, no retro auth, reauth up to 60 calendar days before auth end, the plan must identify the specific services per behavior target, and only one authorized supervisor at a time. The FAQ accepts non-RBT technician credentials (QABA, BCAT), which contradicts the seed rule requiring RBT.
- Regence UMP WA (2014) organizes goals by six domains (social communication, behavior, adaptive, motor, vocational, cognitive), which conflicts with the TRICARE and Cigna vocational exclusions. It requires per-goal discharge criteria plus a referral program, and a separate authorization request form with severity ratings. Use it as structural reference only.
